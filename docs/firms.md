# HFT / Market-Maker Financial Research — Firm Notes
Access / research date: 2026-09-11. Scope: ~2019 through latest available 2026. Prefer primary filings; press leaks for privates. Never invent numbers — holes left explicit.

## Methodology notes
- **Units**: values in millions of original currency unless `unit=count` (headcount).
- **FX**: reused dashboard yearly averages (EURUSD 2019 1.12 … 2025 1.10; GBPUSD 2019 1.28 … 2025 1.32) unless native USD.
- **Conflicts**: both observations retained with notes (e.g. IMC 2023 revenue; Citadel capital $21B vs $21.8B; Flow normalized vs reported).
- **Quarterly vs annual**: annual never labeled as quarterly.

## 1. Jane Street
**Public status**: Private. No statutory public P&L. Occasional debt-investor figures leak via Bloomberg/Reuters.

**What is public**
- Calendar FY2022 net trading revenue ~$10.7B; FY2023 $10.6B; FY2024 $20.5B (+ record NI $13B); FY2025 $39.6B (Bloomberg/Reuters Apr 2026).
- 2025Q4 trading revenue $15.5B (Bloomberg).
- FY ending Mar 2021: $10.6B (do **not** conflate with CY2020).
- Headcount ~3,500 cited with FY2025 haul.

**Missing**
- All other quarters 2019–2026; capital series; systematic headcount; audited statements.
- Pre-2022 calendar years beyond the Mar-2021 FY print.

**Entity caveats**: Jane Street Group LLC. Capital-markets / venture stakes can affect NI (Anthropic etc. noted in Reuters).

## 2. Citadel Securities
**Public status**: Private (debt disclosures to investors). Distinct from Citadel LLC hedge fund.

**What is public**
- Annual NTR: 2020 $6.7B; 2021 $7.0B; 2022 $7.5B; 2023 ~$6.3B; 2024 $9.7B; 2025 $12.2B.
- Some quarters: 2020Q4 $1.7B; 2024Q1 $2.3B; 2025Q3 $2.64B; 2025Q4 $3.8B NTR / $1.7B NI; 2025 YTD9m ~$8.4B.
- FY2025 profit ~$5.4B (rough); trading capital ~$21–21.8B (secondary conflict).

**Missing**: Most quarters 2019–2023; capital time series; headcount; audited GAAP statements.

**Entity caveats**: Market-making arm only. Risk.net / Bloomberg / BT used when primary paywalled.

## 4. XTX Markets

**What is public**

## 7. Virtu Financial (NYSE: VIRT)
**Public status**: Fully public. SEC 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K EX-99.1.

**What is public**
- Complete quarterly **Adjusted Net Trading Income** 2019Q1–2026Q2 and FY totals.
- Quarterly GAAP net income for most quarters 2020Q1–2026Q2; FY2019 loss ~$103m.
- Stockholders’ equity available in 10-K/10-Q (complex NCI structure) — not fully harvested into this pass for every quarter.

**Missing / weak**
- 2019Q1 and 2019Q4 GAAP NI not confidently extracted in this pass → null in gaps.
- Trading capital (regulatory) not always labeled separately from equity.
- Headcount not systematically disclosed each quarter.

**Entity caveats**: ANTI is non-GAAP. Post-ITG acquisition distorts 2019 NI.

## 8. Flow Traders (Euronext: FLOW)
**Public status**: Public. Quarterly/half-year press releases + annual reports.

**What is public**
- Strong NTI / NI / trading capital coverage 2019–2025Q4, with denser data 2023–2025.
- FY2025 NTI €485.8m, NI €133.6m, trading capital €1,044m, 635 FTEs.
- Some 2021–2022 quarters from transcripts / later comparative columns (image-only PDFs blocked text extract).

**Missing**
- 2020Q2 NTI/NI; 2021Q2 NTI/NI; some 2022Q1–Q3 detail; capital for many early quarters.
- Normalized vs IFRS: both series kept where both exist (FY2021/FY2022).

**Entity caveats**: Re-domicile to Bermuda Ltd.; Trading Capital Expansion Plan (dividend suspension) affects capital trajectory from 2024.

## 9. Quadrature Capital (UK 09516131)
**Public status**: Private; **Companies House full accounts** (FY ending 31 Jan).

**What is public**
- Annual turnover, PAT, equity, employees for FY ended 31 Jan 2020–2025.
- FY ended 31 Jan 2025: turnover £1,224.8m, PAT £410.7m, equity £307.9m, 173 employees; large dividend (~£360m).

**Missing**: All quarters; group vs UK-only (immediate parent QC Ventures); trading capital vs equity.

**Entity caveats**: Turnover ≠ trading PNL definition used by market makers; investment income can make PAT ≈ or > turnover in some years. Dividend distortions to retained profit.

## 10. Wolverine Trading
**Public status**: Private broker-dealer. SEC **Form X-17A-5** public portion is **statement of financial condition only** (no income statement).

**What is public**
- Member’s equity YE2024 $362.9m; YE2025 $533.2m.
- Total assets inflated by gross long/short inventory (~$58B assets YE2025) — not economic capital.

**Missing**: **All revenue / net income / trading PNL for all years** — confirmed gap. Growjo/RocketReach “revenue estimates” are third-party guesses — **not used**.

**Entity caveats**: Do not confuse balance-sheet inventory with revenue.
