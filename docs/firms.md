# HFT / Market-Maker Financial Research — Firm Notes
Access / research date: 2026-11-25 (updated from 2026-09-11 baseline). Scope: ~2019 through latest available 2026 (Q3 / 9M / FY2026 where filed). Prefer primary filings; press leaks for privates. Never invent numbers — holes left explicit.

## Methodology notes
- **Units**: values in millions of original currency unless `unit=count` (headcount).
- **FX**: dashboard yearly averages (EURUSD 2019 1.12 … 2025 1.10, 2026 1.10; GBPUSD 2019 1.28 … 2025 1.32, 2026 1.30) plus spreadsheet FX Assumptions for merged 2022–2026 EUR/GBP rows (EURUSD 2026 = 1.1668).
- **Conflicts**: both observations retained with notes (e.g. IMC 2023 revenue; Citadel capital $21B vs $21.8B; Citadel 2024Q3 $2,170m vs derived $2,422m; Flow normalized vs reported).
- **EBITDA vs Net Income**: misclassified EBITDA rows removed from `net_income` during the 2026-11-25 audit (Jane Street 2023 $7.4B adjusted earnings & 2025 $31.2B adjusted EBITDA; HRT 2025Q4 $2.2B EBITDA & FY2025 $8B EBITDA).
